About

NIL is a melee combat game set in post-apocalyptic 2079 industrial Japan. Fight through a world consumed by advanced machines and endless rain using nothing but your trusty umbrella.

Story

When Earth's climate reached a tipping point, humanity created the NIL as a last attempt to restore the environment. The machines succeeded... Then they decided humanity was the greatest threat to Earth's future. Now the last survivors struggle across a ruined Japan divided into rival factions while hunted by the very machines that saved the planet.
