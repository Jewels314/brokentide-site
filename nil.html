PROJECT OVERVIEW

NIL is a narrative-driven action adventure set in post-apocalyptic 2079 Japan. Explore neon-lit ruins reclaimed by overgrowth, evade the biomechanical hunters still patrolling the wreckage, and survive in a city where the rain never stopped falling.

Log_02 // Transmission

THE STORY

Earth's climate collapsed. Humanity created the NIL to restore the world. The machines succeeded... Then they declared humanity the greatest threat to Earth's future. Now the last survivors struggle through a ruined Japan divided by rival factions while hunted by the very machines that once saved them.
